# Harmony Heights visual direction

The page is a live-stage poster translated to the web: a near-black venue backdrop, acid-lime signal color, orange energy hits, oversized display typography, and documentary photography that reveals its color on interaction. The visual language refuses the standard centered band landing page and instead uses a strong typographic entrance, a scrolling venue ticker, and a direct path from discovery to listening, dates, and booking.

- **Mode:** Persuade / Experience
- **Palette:** `#0B0D0E` ink, `#F1F0E9` paper, `#D6FF43` signal lime, `#FF6B3D` stage orange
- **Type:** Syne for expressive display; Space Grotesk for readable UI; DM Mono for metadata
- **Motion:** one reveal system for content entering the viewport, a low-key ticker, and purposeful hover movement on photography
- **Responsive rule:** the desktop collage becomes a single-column stage sequence; the tour list collapses to a stacked reading order on narrow screens
